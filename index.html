The Beautiful, Messy Truth No One Posts on Instagram

We have all seen the idealized version of the 20s online. It is a glossy montage of graduation caps, first apartment keys,
rooftop drinks, and promotions. It looks like a straight line to success, wrapped in a perfect aesthetic.
But if you are actually living in your 20s, you know the truth. The real,unfiltered 20s are a chaotic, confusing, and beautiful mess.
It is a decade where you are expected to act like anadult, but you still feel like a kid trying to figure out how taxes work.
